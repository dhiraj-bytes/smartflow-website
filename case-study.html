The Client

A B2B manufacturer in sports merchandising, selling custom event credentials and bag tags to schools, athletic departments, and youth sports organizations across the country.

The Challenge

The business relied almost entirely on repeat customers and offline referrals — official supplier relationships with sports organizations, word of mouth among athletic directors, and years of reputation. But buyers searching online for exactly what they made never found them. They were invisible to anyone who didn't already know them, and every new customer had to come through an existing relationship.

The Solution

We rebuilt their online presence around how their actual buyers search and decide — not around generic marketing best practices. That meant SEO built for the specific language athletic directors and booster clubs use, a stronger and simpler path from a first visit to a real inquiry, and visibility work so they'd show up when buyers ask AI tools like ChatGPT for recommendations, not just Google.

The Result

Online sales grew 200% in one year, with organic traffic up sharply alongside it. Buyers who'd never heard of them before were now finding them directly — on top of the offline relationships that had always been the business's foundation.
