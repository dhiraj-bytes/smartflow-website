The Client

A 25-year-old Chicago manufacturer of custom event credentials and bag tags — the official credential supplier to USA Swimming, USA Hockey, and Ironman, serving roughly 800 schools, athletic departments, conferences, and youth sports organizations a year.

The Challenge

Twenty-five years had built a trusted name, but almost entirely through relationships. Nearly every order still arrived by phone or email from an existing contact or industry partner. The website drew visitors but rarely turned them into buyers or quote requests — and the higher-margin, self-serve online channel they wanted was barely a channel at all.

  • Effectively invisible for the terms buyers actually search — findable only by people who already knew the brand name
  • No tracking or follow-up process for the inquiries that did come in
  • Little to no presence when buyers asked AI tools like ChatGPT or Gemini for supplier recommendations
The Solution

We rebuilt their online presence around how their actual buyers search and decide — not around generic marketing best practices.

  • Monthly SEO grounded in their real buyers — blog content and landing pages targeting the terms and buying patterns of each customer segment
  • A site redesign that moves buyers from first visit to submitted quote or order in fewer steps
  • AI-visibility work so their pages get cited when buyers ask ChatGPT or Gemini for recommendations, not just ranked on Google
  • Tracking on every inquiry, so online became a measurable channel instead of a black box
The Result

Online sales grew 200% in one year, with organic search traffic up 80% — and organic search became their single largest revenue channel, ahead of direct and paid.

  • AI search visibility climbed from 16% to 43% — the brand is now cited in nearly half the ChatGPT and Gemini queries buyers use to find a supplier
  • 76 keywords now rank on the first page of Google, with more terms reaching the top 3 every quarter
  • Buyers who'd never heard of them were now finding — and citing — them directly
  • Online orders became a real, trackable channel, not a byproduct of existing relationships
"We used to be the best-kept secret in youth sports credentials. Not anymore."
— Brian, Marketing Director, Bagtags
