Why not off-the-shelf

Most business software is a bad deal over time.

You pay per seat forever, use a fraction of the features, and bend your process to fit the tool. For a workflow that's specific to your business, building the right thing once is usually cheaper and always a better fit.

  • Fits your process exactly — not the 80% an off-the-shelf tool covers
  • You pay to build it once, not to rent it per user every month
  • Only the features you need, so your team actually uses it
  • You own the tool and the code
  • It changes when your process changes — no vendor roadmap to wait on
